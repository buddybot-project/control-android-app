## 📱 Overview:
**BuddyBot Control Android App** is an **Android application** designed to control your **BuddyBot** — an intelligent platform featuring voice interaction, facial recognition, facial expressions, emotional feedback, and precise movement.  
The app provides joystick-based control for movement and the camera, displays a video stream from the device, and allows issuing action commands, including speech and expressive responses.
